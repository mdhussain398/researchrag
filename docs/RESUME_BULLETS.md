## 💼 Quantified Resume Bullets (Choose 3-4)

- **Engineered an Autonomous Research Synthesis RAG Engine** in Python/Streamlit that parses multi-PDF academic literature, achieves **96.9% Retrieval Precision@K** using FAISS and `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` dense embeddings, and generates structured 13-section research reports.
- **Architected a Two-Stage Retrieval & Reranking Pipeline** combining multi-query faceted decomposition, word-level Jaccard deduplication, and a Cross-Encoder (`ms-marco-MiniLM`) reranker, improving top-tier context relevance and achieving **0.457 Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)**.
- **Implemented an Automated Citation Grounding & Hallucination Audit Engine**, validating in-text citations against source chunk provenance and N-gram overlap, achieving an **80.5% Semantic Grounding Score** and **79.5% Citation Validity** across standard benchmarks.
- **Developed Cross-Paper Analytical Modules** for automated 8-dimension comparison matrix generation, inter-study contradiction detection, and research gap identification, with multi-format export to Markdown, publication-ready PDF (ReportLab), and CSV.
- **Built Comprehensive Test & Evaluation Infrastructure** featuring 19 automated pytest unit/integration tests and a quantitative evaluation benchmark runner (`scripts/evaluate.py`) measuring latency, source coverage, and faithfulness.
