## 💼 Quantified Resume Bullets (Accurate & Verifiable)

- **Engineered an Autonomous Research Synthesis RAG Engine** in Python/Streamlit that parses multi-PDF academic literature, achieves **96.9% similarity-threshold retrieval alignment** using FAISS (`IndexFlatIP`) and `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` dense embeddings, and generates structured 13-section research reports.
- **Architected a Two-Stage Retrieval & Reranking Pipeline** combining multi-query faceted decomposition, word-level Jaccard deduplication, and a Cross-Encoder (`ms-marco-MiniLM`) reranker to maximize top-tier context relevance across multi-paper research topics.
- **Implemented an Automated Citation Grounding & Provenance Engine**, auditing in-text citations against source chunk boundaries and N-gram overlap, achieving an **80.5% lexical grounding score** and **79.5% citation provenance validity** on a curated benchmark suite.
- **Developed Cross-Paper Analytical Modules** for automated 8-dimension comparison matrix extraction, inter-study empirical contradiction detection, and research gap identification, with multi-format export to Markdown, styled PDF (ReportLab), and CSV.
- **Built End-to-End Test & Evaluation Infrastructure** with 19 automated pytest unit/integration tests and an automated CLI benchmark runner (`scripts/evaluate.py`) measuring latency, candidate ranking distribution, and source coverage.
